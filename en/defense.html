US Approves $2.1 Billion Sale of 500 Patriot Missiles to Ukraine

The United States State Department has approved the potential foreign military sale of 500 Patriot PAC-3 air defense interceptor missiles to Ukraine for an estimated $2.1 billion. The transaction comes amid ongoing tensions between Kyiv and Washington regarding the transfer and delivery timelines of essential military interceptor equipment.

TOTE Services previously won a $2.2 billion contract in July to serve as the primary construction manager for the Landing Ship Medium project, which will ultimately expand the operational capabilities of the naval forces.
