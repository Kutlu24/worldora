BepiColombo Spacecraft Arrives at Mercury to Begin Orbit Mission

After an eight-year journey spanning six billion miles, the joint European and Japanese BepiColombo spacecraft has arrived at Mercury. The mission is set to become only the second in history to enter the planet's orbit. The double probe will map the surface and study the interior of the solar system's smallest and least-studied planet, aiming to shed light on its unusually large core and provide the best-ever view of the world.

Fossils show Homo erectus survived in mainland East Asia much longer than believed

New fossil evidence from southern China indicates that Homo erectus inhabited mainland East Asia until around 324,000 to 184,000 years ago during the late Middle Pleistocene. Previously, fossil records suggested the species had vanished from the region by approximately 400,000 years ago. These findings from the Naxian and Baxian caves help bridge a significant chronological and geographical gap in the understanding of human evolution across the region.
